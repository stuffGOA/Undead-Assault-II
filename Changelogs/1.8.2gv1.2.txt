## Fixes
### Explosion Crash
It is still unclear what was the root cause of the explosion crashes. However, it had been discovered that the crash would normally occur around the 8-minute mark. Several libraries related to the crash had been reworked from the ground up and had seemingly resolved itself after several tests of continuous explosion via chain reaction. Hopefully this fixes it without the fear of crashing a good game.

### Vision
Vision is important! It provides the team an advantage in dealing looming threats that’s headed their way. However, the gameplay aspect is quite lackluster and doesn’t have any depth to it; throw a ground flare in the middle of the forest, surrounded by trees, and it will provide the team an absolute vision around the area. This update aims to change that.
- Damaging abilities and items will no longer provide a brief window of vision of the target area with the exception of Illumination Round and Precision Shot
- Ground flares (ability & item) can be obstructed by the environment
- You will now be able to see White Phosphorus, Smoke, Satchel Charge, Claymore, and C4 in the fog of war.
