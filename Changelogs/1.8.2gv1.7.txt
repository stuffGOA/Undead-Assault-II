### Rifleman
- Automatically defaulting to Rifleman will now be properly start out at level 3
- **Smoke Grenade**

### Player Commands
- **-t**

### Forward Observer
- **-fost**

### M2A3 Bradley

### Default Difficulty
- Fixed infestation not working on defaulting to Normal Mode
### Hit Count
- Fixed hit counter not registering hits correctly
### Plasma Grenade

### Saline Syringe (MOS)
