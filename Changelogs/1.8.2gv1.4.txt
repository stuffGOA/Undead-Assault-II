## Balancing & Changes
### MOS Weapon System
**Shotgun**
- Base reload duration decreased from 4s to 1s
**Weapon Jam at Full Magazine (+1)**
- Reloading with a weapon jam at full magazine with an extra round chambered will only go through the unjamming process
### Global Respawn
- The final global respawn have been moved and will now trigger at the start of the Twin mission (#5th-mission)
### TOW Missile
The homing feature for the TOW missile was causing some unforseen bugs and misuses; such as not wanting to use the homing feature. The TOW missile will now be split into 2 separate abilities, balanced, and renamed.
- **BGM-72A TOW 3N**
- The standard AoE non-homing TOW missile to clear out enemies within an area.
  - Damage per level decreased from 500 to 400
- **BGM-72H TOW 3N**
- The homing TOW missile for individual targets.
  - Will only have a 1-layer damage
  - **Targeted Unit**
    - Base damage increased from 1300 to 1500
    - Damage per level remains the same at 500
  - **Outer Blast**
    - Base damage decreased from 1300 to 450
    - Damage per level decreased from 500 to 150
    - Blast radius decreased from 375 to 256
### Infestation
- Class S and Class A zombies will now receive a **Mutating Metabolism** buff upon entering the map
- **Mutating Metabolism**
  - Prevents the zombie from receiving Evolved Metabolism for 5 minutes
### Satchel Charge
- Satchel Charge will now detonate if destroyed by M2A3 Bradley or M3 Defender

## Gameplay
### Global RXP Reset
Starting in 1.8.2gv1.4, all save codes from all the previous versions will be invalid and will no longer work from here on out. In place of this reset comes with a new save process with some QoL, and preparation for future versions (yes, achievements are coming). 

Due to the generated code being much longer than before, the generated code will no longer show ingame when saving and -load command will no longer be available. With that said, it is highly recommended to make a backup copy of your saved txt file or risk losing all of your progress; you can find your saved txt file at `\Documents\Warcraft III\CustomMapData\Undead Assault 2`, the file name being your `BattleNet User Id` with a `!` at the end.

Code recovery is still an option for both before and after the global RXP reset; however, this DOES NOT mean you can recover your RXP from previous versions to 1.8.2gv1.4, everyone will be starting over from scratch on 1.8.2gv1.4. e.g. 999999 RXP from 1.8.2gv1.3 to 1.8.2gv1.4 is a NO. With that said, it is still highly recommended to make a backup copy for the latter, more on this will be elaborated in the future (possibly on 1.8.3).
- Current view distance number (-v ##) will be saved and carried over to the next game
- Fire Mission & Illumination Round commands ( -fs/-fi/-fr -is/-ii/-ir ) will be saved and carried over to the next game
