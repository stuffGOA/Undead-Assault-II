# 1.8.2gv1.3
## Fixes
### High Pings
- Discovered an audio process that was causing the gradual increase in ping, that has been disabled for the time being
### Kill Counter
- Kill counter will show the correct amount of enemy kills
### Umbrella Ping
- Fixed an issue where Scout Reconnaissance level 1 will activate the periodic Umbrella position ping
### Field Camera
- Fixed an issue where player placed Field Cameras will share vision to the Umbrella
### Weapon Sound Effects
- Fixed an issue where picking up an SA Combat Shotgun or a G89 will play their respective SFX regardless of what MOS the player are playing as
### Detonating Satchel Charges
- Fixed an issue where placing a Satchel Charge inside of an ultimate ability AoE will not detonate. i.e. Placing a Satchel Charge inside the AoE of an incoming Air Strike
